# SORE! Second Opinion Required: Emergency

Interactive anatomy case studies for high school students. Take the history, check the vitals, order only what you need, and make the diagnosis without harming the patient.

**Play:** https://anatomyacademy101.github.io/sore/

- Unit 1: Patient history & vital signs
- Unit 2: The skeletal system
- Organ systems (preview)

Students turn in a PDF case report through Google Classroom.

Educational simulation, not medical advice. Patients are fictional.
